🚀 Mission overview

NASA Artemis program — back to the Moon
Mission I
Artemis I
Nov 16 – Dec 11, 2022
Uncrewed test flight of SLS and Orion. The capsule flew 450,000 km from Earth and passed the Moon at a distance of 130 km. Splashdown on December 11, 2022.
✓ Completed
Mission II
Artemis II
April 2026
First crewed flight — four astronauts (Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch, Jeremy Hansen). Free-return trajectory around the Moon without landing.
✓ Completed
Mission III
Artemis III
Planned ~2027
First crewed Moon landing since Apollo 17 (1972). Astronauts will land using the SpaceX Starship HLS at the Moon's South Pole. SLS hardware is now arriving at Kennedy.
● In preparation
Mission IV
Artemis IV
Planned ~2028+
First mission with the Lunar Gateway — a space station orbiting the Moon. Longer stays on the lunar surface and expansion of the Gateway modules.
◯ Planned
